Ascend Darts Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Ascend Darts stores gameplay data locally on your device. The app may show ads via Google AdMob and offers a Remove Ads in-app purchase through Apple.

We may request App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS. If you deny permission, tracking-based personalization is limited accordingly.
