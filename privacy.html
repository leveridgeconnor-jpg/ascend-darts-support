Ascend Darts - Privacy Policy

How Ascend Darts handles information for the iOS app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email us at levandlevltd@outlook.com.

Information we collect

  • On-device game data - Career progress, settings, and local saves are stored on your device.
  • Advertising data (AdMob) - Google AdMob may process identifiers, approximate location, interaction data, and diagnostics for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • In-app purchase data - Apple processes payment for the Remove Ads purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, we may request tracking permission through App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, tracking-based personalization is limited accordingly.

How we use information

We use information to run the app, save gameplay progress, provide and measure ads, process and restore purchases, and improve app reliability.

Third-party services

We use Google AdMob and Apple App Store services. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until removed by you (for example, by deleting the app). Third-party retention follows each provider's own policies.

Children

Ascend Darts is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will review.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Last updated: May 5, 2026.
