Ascend Darts — Support

Official support page for the Ascend Darts iOS app.

Contact

For help, bug reports, or feedback, email us at levandlevltd@outlook.com. Please include your device model (e.g. iPhone 15) and iOS version (Settings → General → About).

Common questions

  • Remove Ads — If you purchased Remove Ads, it is tied to your Apple ID. If it doesn’t appear restored, contact us with your purchase details and we’ll help you troubleshoot.
  • Saves — Career progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove local saves.
  • Privacy — See the Privacy Policy link in the app or on the App Store product page when provided.
